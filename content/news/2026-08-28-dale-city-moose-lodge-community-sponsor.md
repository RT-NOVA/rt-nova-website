Rawlings Tigers NOVA is proud to welcome **Dale City Moose Lodge 2165** as a community sponsor.

This support represents an investment in local athletes, teams, and families. Community sponsorships help the NOVA Tigers Booster Club strengthen the player experience and provide resources that support development, team needs, and opportunities throughout the program.

Dale City Moose Lodge 2165 has a strong tradition of service and community involvement. We are grateful that the Lodge has chosen to support the Tigers and help us continue building a positive baseball community across Northern Virginia.

Thank you to the members of Dale City Moose Lodge 2165 for standing behind our players and families. We are excited to welcome you to the Tigers family.
